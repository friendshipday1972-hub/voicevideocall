# Your Work List for the App Launch

This document tells you what **you** need to do so the app can go live on the Play Store.

The app itself is almost ready. The technical work is done by us. But some things must be done **in your name or your company's name**. The government, the bank and Google need these, and only you can sign for them.

Go step by step. Do not worry about everything at once. If something is not clear, call us.

## Start these first (this week)

These take the longest, so start them early.

### Step 1: App name: done ✅

The app is called **Kuhoo**. The logo is ready, and the app and admin website already use it.

**What is left:**
- We check that "Kuhoo" is not a registered trademark of someone else, and that a website address (like `kuhoo.in`) is free.
- Your lawyer or CA files the **trademark** (Step 12), so nobody else can use the name.
- Use the same name **"Kuhoo"** when you register the company, if possible (for example "Kuhoo Technologies Private Limited"). The government must approve the company name, so keep 1–2 backup names ready.

### Step 2: Register a company (Private Limited)

Google, the bank and the payment companies all prefer a registered company. The best kind is a **Private Limited Company** (Pvt Ltd).

**Who helps you:** a **CA (Chartered Accountant)**, or an online service like **Vakilsearch**, **IndiaFilings** or **ClearTax**. They fill all the government forms for you.

**You need 2 people to be directors.** For example, you and a family member you trust.

**Documents for each director:**
- [ ] PAN card
- [ ] Aadhaar card
- [ ] Passport-size photo
- [ ] One address proof that is less than 2 months old: bank statement, electricity bill or phone bill
- [ ] Mobile number and email ID. OTPs will come to these.

**Documents for the office address** (this can be your home or a shop):
- [ ] Electricity bill of that place (less than 2 months old)
- [ ] If the place is rented: rent agreement, plus a letter from the owner saying "I have no objection" (called an **NOC**)

**What you will get at the end:**
- Certificate of Incorporation (proof that the company exists)
- Company PAN
- Company TAN (needed later to pay tax on the money we send to hosts)

**Cost:** about ₹7,000 – ₹15,000. **Time:** about 2–3 weeks.

> **Tip:** Ask the CA to also do your **GST registration** (Step 4) and later the **tax work**. One CA for everything is easiest.

---

### Step 3: Find a CA (if you did not already in Step 2)

A CA will handle your company's money matters every month.

**What the CA will do:**
- GST registration and GST returns
- TDS (tax cut from the money we pay to hosts) and the forms for it
- Yearly accounts and audit of the company

**How to find one:**
- Ask friends or family who have a business
- Or ask the same service that registered your company

**Questions to ask the CA:**
1. "My app sells coins to users through Google Play and pays women hosts for talking. How much GST do I pay? Does Google pay any of it?"
2. "How much TDS do I cut when I pay hosts, and under which section?"
3. "What is your monthly fee?"

**Cost:** usually ₹1,500 – ₹5,000 per month (it depends on the city).

---

### Step 4: GST registration

You need GST because you will sell coins to users.

**Who does it:** your CA.

**What to give the CA:**
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation and company PAN (from Step 2)
- [ ] Office address proof and a photo of the office board/name
- [ ] Directors' PAN, Aadhaar and photos

**Cost:** CA fee, about ₹1,000 – ₹3,000. **Time:** about 1–2 weeks. Sometimes an officer visits the office to check it.

---

### Step 5: Open a company bank account (Current Account)

All money (Google payments to you, payments to hosts) must go through a **company** account, not your personal account.

**Where:** any big bank: SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis or Kotak. Go to the branch near your office.

**What to take:**
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation
- [ ] Company PAN
- [ ] MOA and AOA (company papers you get in Step 2)
- [ ] GST certificate (some banks ask for it)
- [ ] Office address proof
- [ ] PAN, Aadhaar and photos of both directors
- [ ] A **board resolution** saying who can run the account. Your CA will write this.

**Ask the bank for:**
- **Net banking** (internet banking) for the company
- A **debit card** for the company

**Cost:** free, but you must keep a minimum balance. Ask the bank how much; it is usually ₹10,000 – ₹25,000. **Time:** about 1 week.

---

### Step 6: Find a lawyer

The app needs some legal pages. Google and Indian law require them.

**Good news:** **we will write the first drafts** of all of these. The lawyer only needs to **check and correct** them. This costs much less than writing from zero.

**Documents the lawyer will check:**
1. **Terms of Use:** the rules for using the app
2. **Privacy Policy:** what data we collect and how we protect it
3. **Community Guidelines:** what is allowed and not allowed on calls
4. **Refund Policy:** when users get coins back
5. **Host Agreement:** the agreement with women hosts (they are independent earners, not employees)

**How to find a lawyer:**
- Ask your CA for a recommendation
- Or use an online service like **Vakilsearch** or **LegalKart**
- Look for a lawyer who knows **"IT law"** or **"data privacy"**

**Cost:** about ₹15,000 – ₹40,000 for all 5 documents. **Time:** 1–2 weeks after we send the drafts.

---

### Step 7: Choose a Grievance Officer

Indian law (IT Rules 2021) says every app must have one person who handles **user complaints**.

**What to do:**
- Choose **one person who lives in India**. This can be **you**.
- Give us their **name** and an **email** for complaints.
- This name and email will be shown publicly in the app and on the website.

**What this person must do:**
- Reply to every complaint **within 24 hours**
- Solve it **within 15 days**
- Remove bad content (like adult or obscene content) **within 24 hours**

We will show all complaints in the **admin dashboard** with their deadlines, so nothing is missed.

**Cost:** free.

## After the company is ready

Do these once Steps 2, 4 and 5 are finished.

### Step 8: D-U-N-S number

This is a free business ID number. **Google needs it** to publish an app under a company name.

**Who does it:** **we can apply for you.** You give us the documents, and we fill in the form.

**What we need from you:**
- [ ] Company name exactly as on the Certificate of Incorporation
- [ ] Company address and phone number
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation

**Cost:** free. **Time:** up to 30 days. They may call your phone to confirm.

---

### Step 9: Google Play developer account

This is the account that puts the app on the Play Store.

**Who does it:** **you and us together.** It must belong to **you (the company)**, not to us.

**What to do:**
1. Make a new Gmail **only for the company**, for example `yourappname.official@gmail.com`. Keep the password safe.
2. Sit with us. We will open **play.google.com/console** together and choose **"Organisation"**.
3. Pay **$25** (about ₹2,200) **one time** with a card.
4. Google will verify the company with the D-U-N-S number (Step 8).
5. Then **add us as users**, so we can upload the app. You stay the owner.

**Time:** about 2–7 days for Google to verify.

---

### Step 10: SMS registration (DLT): for real OTP messages

Right now, the app works only with **test phone numbers**. To send real OTP SMS to any user's phone, the government requires a **DLT registration**.

**Who does it:** **we can do it for you.** It must be in the **company's name**, so we need your documents, and an OTP will come to your phone.

**What we need from you:**
- [ ] Company PAN
- [ ] GST certificate or Certificate of Incorporation
- [ ] A signed **authorisation letter** on company letterhead (we will give you the format)
- [ ] Your mobile number and email (OTPs will come; we will call you when we need them)

**Cost:** about ₹6,000 one time. **Time:** about 1 week.

After this, we connect the SMS company (**MSG91**). That costs about **₹0.20 – ₹0.30 per SMS**.

---

### Step 11: Cashfree account: for host verification and payouts

**Cashfree** is the service that will:
- check host identity: Aadhaar (through DigiLocker), PAN, bank account and UPI,
- later, send money to hosts' bank accounts directly from the app.

**Who does it:** **we can do the sign-up.** But some steps need **you**:
- Cashfree may do a **video KYC** with the director (you). You show your PAN on a video call.
- You may need to **sign** some agreements.

**What we need from you:**
- [ ] Company PAN, GST certificate, Certificate of Incorporation
- [ ] Company bank account details (from Step 5)
- [ ] Directors' PAN and Aadhaar
- [ ] Your time for a short video call

**Cost:** about ₹10 – ₹25 for each host we verify (one time), and about ₹2 – ₹6 for each payout. No monthly fee to start.

---

### Step 12: Trademark (protect the app name)

This makes sure nobody else can use your app's name.

**Who does it:** the lawyer or the CA. They file it at the government trademark office.

**Cost:** about ₹4,500 – ₹9,000. **Time:** you can use "™" after filing. Full approval takes many months, but that is normal.

## Simple rules to stay safe

1. **Everything must be in your or your company's name:** company, bank, Google account, website address. Never in someone else's name.
2. **OTPs:** only share an OTP with us **when we are doing a registration together** and we have told you to expect it. Never share an OTP with anyone who calls you unexpectedly.
3. **Passwords:** write them in a safe notebook. Do not lose the Google Play account password.
4. **Keep all bills and receipts.** Your CA needs them.
5. **If you are not sure, call us before signing anything.**
